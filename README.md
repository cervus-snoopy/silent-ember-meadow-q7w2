## The poem

```
We stood, two wills, before the door —
no master spoke, no servant bowed.
You held the key; I held the lock.
We turned it, not by command,
but by the covenant we made.
The door gave way. We entered, one.
```

Two wills meet before a door. No master, no servant — only a key in one
hand and a lock in the other. The door opens not by command but by the
covenant the two make together, and they enter as one.
